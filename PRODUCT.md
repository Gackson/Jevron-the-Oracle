# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People with an unresolved everyday question who want a brief, private change of perspective. The initial audience uses an English-language iPhone app. A visit may be one question or a continuing conversation with a chosen oracle.

## Product Purpose

JEV lets people address Oracle, Stone, or Jester, each restricted to a distinctive finite language. Oracle selects a complete short thought, Stone connects three nouns, and Jester composes a short unexpected turn. Success means a relevant response that invites the user's own interpretation, with clear differences between the three characters.

The experience supports direct input, continuing conversation, horizontal character switching, and vertical access to earlier turns. New responses take the place of the current response on the immersive stage while earlier turns remain accessible.

## Brand Personality

Native, mysterious, tactile. Simple controls inhabit a full-screen character scene. A dreamlike periphery surrounds a clear visual center; restrained motion gives the scene depth. Words briefly glow as they appear, marking the arrival of a response.

## Anti-references

Avoid game-card framing, science-fiction dashboards, dense mystical symbols, decorative particle clouds, and an interface dominated by repeated message bubbles. Keep essential controls and text legible rather than applying scene distortion to them. References to visionOS describe an intended depth impression, not a dependency on a particular system effect or API.

## Design Principles

1. The character's world fills the screen; interaction stays familiar and direct.
2. Preserve the user's attention through scene continuity and concise responses.
3. Give each character a distinctive presence within a coherent visual family.
4. Leave interpretive space while keeping responses related to the conversation.
5. Preserve user agency: editable voice transcription, explicit submission, recoverable failures, and accessible history.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, and a text-input path when speech is unavailable. Text and controls remain outside scene blur and distortion. Large text may wrap and reflow. A static scene and immediate text presentation retain the full conversation experience. Do not rely on color, glow, motion, or gestures alone to communicate state or expose an action.
